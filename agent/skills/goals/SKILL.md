---
name: goals
description: Register sustained work, interpret explicit time budgets, review progress independently and deliver a final report.
---

Use the shared `goal` tool when the user explicitly requests sustained work, including ordinary language or `/goal`. Do not create a goal for every ordinary task. Bind the objective and observable completion criteria to the authenticated source input. Interpret “work for three hours” as a timebox with a report at its end; “at least three hours” as a floor; “up to three hours” as a cap; and an explicit stop time as a deadline. A reporting checkpoint is distinct from a stop budget. Explain a material ambiguity once before dependent work.

Keep the objective, user corrections and report obligation durable. Record actual progress, remaining requirements, verified evidence and unresolved external outcomes. Resume preserves accrued usage. A new cap or extension requires a new authenticated authorization; ambient Slack observations cannot extend or reactivate work.

When reviewing completion, compare each requirement to original evidence. A successful tool call or a worker saying “done” is insufficient. Use the independent review result and retrieve its requested source records. On a blocker, reconsider alternatives within the authorized scope before deciding that user input is necessary. New direct inputs invalidate a stale completion assessment.

At a timebox/cap/deadline, stop launching substantive work and settle the reporting phase. Save artifacts and evidence, account for in-flight/uncertain operations and finished child results, then report completed and verified work, remaining work, blockers, usable outputs, actual usage and a resumption point. Budget exhaustion is not completion. If report inference fails, preserve the factual fallback and delivery obligation; never disappear silently or fabricate a successful assessment.
